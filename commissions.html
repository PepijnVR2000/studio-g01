Commission

We design a ceramic piece for one wall, room, shop or museum display and print it in our studio in Delft. For architects, interior architects and museums.

  • A wallTiles or a relief, made to its size.
  • A roomOne piece or a family of pieces for an interior.
  • A shopObjects or a display for a brand's space.
  • A museum displayAn installation around a theme or a collection.

Collaboration

With brands, makers and researchers we develop a collection, a material or a question. We bring the robot and the record of every attempt; you bring your own field.

  • A collectionA series with a brand or a maker, printed in Delft.
  • ResearchA question about material, form or climate, tested attempt by attempt.
  • A materialA clay body or a waste stream, developed until it prints.
